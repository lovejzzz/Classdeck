# Running a class without outside tools

Classdeck can do everything a Google Form did: get students onto their own board, and collect feedback after an activity. Nothing here needs email, an outside form, or a paid service.

## Getting students in

Open **Settings → Students joining**.

- **Show to the class** puts a large QR code and the class code on screen. Students scan it (or go to the site and type the code), then either pick their name from your list or add themselves, and choose a 4-digit PIN. After that they sign in with the class code, their name, and their PIN.
- **Who can join** decides what happens to students who aren't on your list:
  - *Only my list*: they can't add themselves.
  - *Anyone, with my OK*: they add themselves and wait. You see them under **Waiting to be let in** (and a dot on the Settings tab) and choose **Let in** or **Turn away**. Their board opens by itself as soon as you let them in.
  - *Anyone, straight in*: they add themselves and start right away.
- A name already on the list can't be added again, so a student who is on the list picks their name instead.
- New students are put in the smallest Collab Studio group.

### Private setup links

Picking a name from the list is quick, but anyone with the class code could pick a classmate's name before that classmate has set a PIN. To stop that, choose **Make links** under **Private setup links**. Every student on your list who hasn't signed in yet gets a one-time link to their own board. Until they use it, no one can claim their name with the class code.

- **Print slips** prints a slip per student with their name and a QR code. Cut them up and hand them out.
- **Copy all** copies name, email (if known), and link, for an email merge.
- Each link works once and expires after 14 days. Making links again replaces the old ones.

The join link and QR code use this site's address. On a copy running on your own computer they only work on that computer; once Classdeck is online, they point to the public address.

## Forms on cards

Any card except a wall can have a form: one you fill in for each student (for example after a workshop or a practice talk), or one each student fills in themselves.

1. Open the card in the Deck and find **Your form for this card → Add a form**.
2. Name it ("Shown to students as"), choose what it asks for (feedback, try next, skill levels, a score out of a total, links, done / revise), and add your own questions. A question with choices gets buttons; without choices it gets a short written answer.
3. In the **Class** tab, open a student's cell for that card. The form is in the side panel. **Save and next** saves and moves to the next student who has the card, so you can go down the class quickly.

What the student sees: your answers on the card itself and in their feedback inbox. Skill levels count on their skill chart like a review. "Mark done" finishes the card for them; "Ask to revise" hands it back with your feedback as the reason. Links are added to their work on that card.

Filling in a student's form again replaces your earlier answers rather than adding a second set. A small form icon in the Class grid shows whose forms you've done.

Each student only ever receives their own answers.

### Forms students fill in

Under **Who fills it in**, choose **Each student** instead of **Me, for each student**. Use it for a reflection after a workshop, a check-in, or a quick survey.

- Write your questions. A question with choices gets buttons; without choices it gets a written answer. You can also ask students to **rate themselves** (1–4 on the card's skills) and to add **links** (recordings, slides).
- Students see the form on the card and choose **Send**. They can come back and change their answers; the new ones replace the old.
- **Sending it finishes the card** is on by default. Turn it off if the card has other work to hand in.
- In the **Class** tab, the lesson has a table of everyone's answers, with who hasn't sent theirs yet, and **Download CSV**. Opening a student's cell shows their answers too, and a small form icon marks who has sent theirs.
- Self-ratings count on the student's skill chart as their own rating. Links are added to their work on that card.
- Only you see a student's answers. Classmates never do.

## Google Forms is still there

If you already use Google Forms, [the Google Forms connection](google-forms.md) keeps working. Answers from it look the same to students.
